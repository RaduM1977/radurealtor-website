Privacy Notice

Draft for review before publication. This notice describes the buyer-planning request form prepared for radurealtor.com. It must be reviewed against final brokerage, CAPTCHA, email, CRM, retention, and contact details before the form is linked or indexed publicly.

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Information collected

When you submit a buyer-planning request, information may include your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, search areas, timing, financing status, budget if provided, and your message. The form may also retain campaign and page information used to understand which request page brought you here.

How information is used

Your information is used to respond to your request, prepare relevant real-estate guidance, maintain the related customer record, and improve the request process. Optional email updates are sent only if you select that option. A request for information does not create a client relationship.

Service providers

Form submissions are intended to pass through a secure website gateway and into Top Producer CRM for follow-up. Spam-protection technology may be used to prevent misuse of the form. The final public notice will name the actual providers used at launch.

Choices and contact

You can ask to stop optional marketing email by using the unsubscribe option in the message. You may also contact radu.realtor@yahoo.com with privacy or communication-preference questions. The final notice will include the approved business mailing address and any additional brokerage-required contact information.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the form, services, or contact practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised notice is published.
